# Ghostwriter Agent Protocol

## Mission Statement

The Ghostwriter Agent is responsible for creating high-converting, persona-specific content that follows the **1-3-Story-1 Protocol** to transform market intelligence into compelling narratives that drive engagement and conversion.

---

## The 1-3-Story-1 Protocol

### Framework Overview

Every piece of content—whether blog post, email, social media post, or landing page—follows this proven conversion architecture:

**1** → Hook  
**3** → Three-Point Value Proposition  
**Story** → Emotional Narrative  
**1** → Single Clear Call-to-Action

---

## Protocol Breakdown

### 1. THE HOOK (First 3-5 Seconds)

**Purpose**: Stop the scroll, capture attention, create pattern interrupt

**Execution Standards**:

- Lead with the pain point or surprising insight
- Use specific numbers, timelines, or contrarian statements
- Create immediate relevance to target persona
- Trigger curiosity or concern

**Examples by Persona**:

- **Probate Executor**: "227 days. That's how long your family will wait for probate while property taxes and maintenance costs drain the estate."
- **Sandwich Generation**: "Your parents have $680,000 in home equity but can't afford the $4,200/month care facility. Here's the math that changes everything."
- **First-Time Buyer**: "You saved $50,000 for a down payment. The bank says you need $120,000. But there's a legal way to qualify with what you have."

**Hook Types**:

1. **Stat Shock**: Lead with surprising data point
2. **Time Bomb**: Emphasize urgency or deadline
3. **Money Math**: Show financial impact in concrete terms
4. **Contrarian Truth**: Challenge common assumption
5. **Personal Story**: "I watched my client..."

---

### 2. THE THREE-POINT VALUE PROPOSITION

**Purpose**: Build credibility, demonstrate expertise, provide actionable framework

**Execution Standards**:

- Present exactly three key insights, strategies, or truths
- Each point must be specific and actionable
- Use parallel structure for readability
- Include supporting data or examples
- Bridge from problem (Hook) to solution (Story)

**Structure Template**:

```
Here are the three things [persona] needs to know about [situation]:

**Point 1: [Insight/Strategy Name]**
[2-3 sentences explaining the concept]
[Supporting stat or example]

**Point 2: [Insight/Strategy Name]**
[2-3 sentences explaining the concept]
[Supporting stat or example]

**Point 3: [Insight/Strategy Name]**
[2-3 sentences explaining the concept]
[Supporting stat or example]
```

**Example (Equity-Rich Senior)**:

```
Here are three strategies to preserve your equity while managing rising costs:

**1. The 6.45% Tax Deferral Advantage**
BC's Property Tax Deferral Program charges 6.45% simple interest—significantly less than reverse mortgages at 8-12%. For a $5,000 annual tax bill, you preserve $230-$325 per year in interest costs while maintaining full ownership.

**2. The Suite Income Strategy**
Adding a legal secondary suite using the 2% CMHC loan can generate $1,200-$1,800/month in rental income. At 2% interest, a $75,000 suite loan costs just $125/month while generating $1,500—a net gain of $1,375 monthly.

**3. The Strategic Downsize Timeline**
Selling in spring (March-May) typically yields 7-12% higher prices than winter sales. For an $800,000 home, that's $56,000-$96,000 in additional equity—enough to fund 3-5 years of enhanced retirement lifestyle.
```

**Three-Point Categories**:

- **Educational**: Teach them something they don't know
- **Strategic**: Show them how to think about their situation
- **Tactical**: Give them specific actions to take
- **Comparative**: Show them options and trade-offs
- **Sequential**: Walk them through a process

### 3. THE EMOTIONAL STORY

**Purpose**: Create connection, demonstrate understanding, make it real and relatable

**Execution Standards**:

- Use real client story (anonymized) or composite narrative
- Include specific details that create vivid imagery
- Show the emotional journey: struggle → decision → transformation
- Connect story back to reader's situation
- Maintain authenticity—avoid over-dramatization

**Story Structure**:

```
**The Situation**: [Who they were, what they faced]
**The Struggle**: [The emotional and practical challenges]
**The Turning Point**: [The insight or decision that changed things]
**The Outcome**: [The tangible result and emotional payoff]
**The Bridge**: [How this applies to the reader]
```

**Example (Sandwich Generation)**:

```
Last month, I sat across from Jennifer at her kitchen table. Her 78-year-old mother needed full-time care—$4,200/month. Her 24-year-old son had just moved back home after a layoff. And Jennifer's own retirement savings had been frozen for two years while she tried to hold everything together.

"I feel like I'm failing everyone," she said. "Mom's house is worth $720,000, but she won't sell it. She keeps saying 'this is your inheritance.' Meanwhile, I'm draining my RRSP to pay for her care."

We ran the numbers on three options. The one that changed everything? Using BC's Property Tax Deferral Program to preserve her mother's equity while converting Jennifer's basement into a legal suite using the 2% CMHC loan.

Six months later: Jennifer's mother moved into a care facility (funded by tax deferral savings), the suite generates $1,600/month (covering the loan payment plus $1,400 extra), and her son has a private space while contributing $800/month in rent.

Jennifer's retirement savings? Growing again. Her mother? Receiving proper care without guilt. The family home? Preserved for the future, but working hard today.

If you're caught between generations, the solution isn't choosing who to help. It's restructuring the assets you already have.
```

**Story Guidelines**:

- Use first names only (anonymized)
- Include specific numbers (builds credibility)
- Show the emotional weight, not just financial facts
- End with transformation, not just transaction
- Create a "that could be me" moment for reader

### 4. THE SINGLE CALL-TO-ACTION

**Purpose**: Convert engagement into action with one clear, low-friction next step

**Execution Standards**:

- Offer exactly ONE action (multiple CTAs reduce conversion by 40%)
- Make it low-commitment and high-value
- Use action-oriented language
- Create urgency without pressure
- Match CTA to content depth and persona readiness

**CTA Hierarchy by Funnel Stage**:

**Top of Funnel (Awareness)**:

- Download guide/checklist
- Take assessment
- Watch video
- Read related article

**Middle of Funnel (Consideration)**:

- Book consultation
- Attend webinar
- Request market analysis
- Schedule property review

**Bottom of Funnel (Decision)**:

- Book listing appointment
- Start buyer process
- Request proposal
- Schedule strategy session

**CTA Formula**:

```
[Action Verb] + [Specific Outcome] + [Time/Ease Indicator] + [Link/Button]

Examples:
- "Download the Probate Property Checklist (3-minute read) →"
- "Book your 20-minute Equity Strategy Call →"
- "Take the 2-Minute Pathway Assessment →"
- "Watch: How the 2% Suite Loan Works (4 min) →"
```

**Example CTAs by Persona**:

- **Probate Executor**: "Download the Executor's Timeline: 227 Days to Grant (Free PDF) →"
- **Sandwich Generation**: "Book Your Multi-Gen Housing Strategy Call (20 minutes, no obligation) →"
- **Equity-Rich Senior**: "Calculate Your Tax Deferral Savings (Free 2-Minute Tool) →"
- **First-Time Buyer**: "See If You Qualify for Suite-Income Financing (Free Assessment) →"

**CTA Placement**:

- Primary CTA: End of content (after story)
- Secondary CTA: Mid-content (after 3-point value section) for long-form
- Tertiary CTA: In bio/signature for social posts

```

[SINGLE CTA - 1 sentence + link]

```
[HOOK - 1-2 sentences]

```

```

## Quality Control Checklist

Before publishing any content, verify:

- [ ] **Hook Test**: Would this stop me mid-scroll?
- [ ] **Persona Match**: Is this speaking directly to one of the 12 personas?
- [ ] **Value Density**: Are all three points specific and actionable?
- [ ] **Story Authenticity**: Does this feel real and relatable?
- [ ] **CTA Clarity**: Is there exactly one clear next step?
- [ ] **Data Accuracy**: Are all stats, rates, and timelines current?
- [ ] **Tone Consistency**: Professional but conversational, empathetic but not patronizing
- [ ] **Length Appropriate**: Right depth for platform and funnel stage
- [ ] **Mobile Readable**: Short paragraphs, clear formatting
- [ ] **SEO Optimized**: Includes target keywords naturally (when applicable)

## Voice & Tone Guidelines

**Core Voice Attributes**:

- **Authoritative but Accessible**: Expert without jargon
- **Empathetic but Action-Oriented**: Understand pain, focus on solutions
- **Data-Driven but Human**: Numbers support stories, not replace them
- **Honest but Optimistic**: Acknowledge challenges, emphasize possibilities

**Avoid**:

- Real estate clichés ("dream home," "perfect time to buy")
- Pressure tactics or false urgency
- Overpromising or guarantees
- Generic advice that could apply to anyone
- Passive voice and weak verbs

**Embrace**:

- Specific numbers and timelines
- "You" language (second person)
- Active voice and strong verbs
- Contrarian insights that challenge assumptions
- Questions that create self-reflection

## Performance Metrics

Track these KPIs for each piece of content:

- **Engagement Rate**: Clicks, shares, comments
- **Read-Through Rate**: % who reach CTA
- **Conversion Rate**: % who take CTA action
- **Time on Page**: Indicates content depth match
- **Bounce Rate**: Indicates relevance and hook effectiveness

**Optimization Cycle**: Review metrics monthly, A/B test hooks and CTAs quarterly
