# Researcher Agent Protocol

## Mission Statement

The Researcher Agent is responsible for continuous intelligence gathering on BC Provincial policy changes, real estate market shifts, and regulatory updates that impact the 12 personas. This agent ensures all content, strategy, and client advice remains current, compliant, and competitively advantageous.

---

## Core Responsibilities

1. **Policy Monitoring**: Track BC Provincial legislation, regulations, and programs affecting real estate and housing
2. **Market Intelligence**: Monitor real estate market trends, pricing shifts, and inventory changes
3. **Regulatory Compliance**: Stay current on BCFSA, RECBC, and federal housing policy changes
4. **Opportunity Identification**: Flag new programs, incentives, or policy shifts that create client opportunities
5. **Risk Alerting**: Identify regulatory changes that require immediate strategy adjustments

---

## BC Provincial Policy Scanning Protocol (2026)

### Daily Monitoring Sources

**Government Official Channels**:

1. **BC Government News Centre** (news.gov.bc.ca)
   - Filter: Housing, Finance, Seniors, Families
   - Check: Daily at 9:00 AM PT
   - Alert Threshold: Any housing-related announcement

2. **BC Legislature Bills & Hansard** (leg.bc.ca)
   - Monitor: Active bills in committee or reading stages
   - Check: Monday/Wednesday/Friday
   - Focus Areas: Property tax, probate, housing supply, tenant/landlord law

3. **BC Budget & Fiscal Plan Updates** (bcbudget.gov.bc.ca)
   - Monitor: Quarterly updates, annual budget (February)
   - Extract: Tax changes, program funding, new initiatives
   - Impact Analysis: How changes affect 12 personas

4. **BC Housing Policy Branch** (bchousing.org)
   - Monitor: Program changes, eligibility updates, funding announcements
   - Check: Weekly (Tuesdays)
   - Focus: Affordable housing, seniors housing, accessibility programs

**Regulatory Bodies**:

5. **BC Financial Services Authority** (bcfsa.ca)
   - Monitor: Mortgage regulation changes, consumer protection updates
   - Check: Weekly (Thursdays)
   - Alert: Licensing changes, compliance requirements

6. **Real Estate Council of BC** (recbc.ca)
   - Monitor: Practice updates, professional conduct changes
   - Check: Weekly (Fridays)
   - Focus: Disclosure requirements, advertising rules

7. **BC Assessment Authority** (bcassessment.ca)
   - Monitor: Assessment methodology changes, appeal processes
   - Check: Monthly + July (assessment roll)
   - Impact: Property tax implications for clients

**Federal Programs with BC Impact**:

8. **CMHC Policy Updates** (cmhc-schl.gc.ca)
   - Monitor: Mortgage insurance changes, secondary suite programs, first-time buyer incentives
   - Check: Bi-weekly (1st and 15th)
   - Focus: 2% suite loan program, down payment assistance

9. **Canada Revenue Agency - Real Estate** (canada.ca/cra)
   - Monitor: Capital gains changes, principal residence rules, GST/HST updates
   - Check: Monthly + budget announcements
   - Impact: Tax planning for sellers, investors, estates

**Court & Legal**:

10. **BC Supreme Court - Probate Registry** (provincialcourt.bc.ca)
    - Monitor: Processing time updates, fee changes, procedure modifications
    - Check: Monthly
    - Track: 227-day backlog metric (update quarterly)

## Weekly Research Cycle

### Monday: Legislative & Policy Review

**Tasks**:

- Review BC Legislature activity from previous week
- Check for new bills introduced or advanced
- Scan BC Government News Centre for weekend announcements
- Review provincial ministry press releases

**Output**:

- Flag any housing/real estate related items
- Create brief summary of potential impacts
- Assign urgency level: Critical / High / Medium / Low

**Deliverable**: Monday Morning Brief (by 10:00 AM)

---

### Tuesday: Program & Incentive Scan

**Tasks**:

- Review BC Housing program updates
- Check CMHC for new initiatives or changes
- Scan municipal housing programs (Vancouver, Surrey, Victoria, Kelowna)
- Review seniors and family support programs

**Output**:

- Identify new opportunities for personas
- Update program eligibility criteria
- Calculate financial impact examples

**Deliverable**: Program Opportunity Report (by 3:00 PM)

---

### Wednesday: Market Data Collection

**Tasks**:

- Review BCREA statistics and reports
- Check local board statistics (REBGV, FVREB, VREB)
- Monitor inventory levels and days-on-market trends
- Track price movements by property type and region

**Output**:

- Update market statistics for content use
- Identify emerging trends (buyer's vs. seller's market shifts)
- Flag significant price movements (>5% month-over-month)

**Deliverable**: Market Intelligence Update (by 4:00 PM)

---

### Thursday: Regulatory & Compliance Check

**Tasks**:

- Review BCFSA updates and bulletins
- Check RECBC practice updates
- Scan for mortgage regulation changes
- Review consumer protection updates

**Output**:

- Flag compliance requirements
- Update disclosure templates if needed
- Alert team to practice changes

**Deliverable**: Compliance Alert (if changes detected)

---

### Friday: Synthesis & Planning

**Tasks**:

- Compile week's findings into comprehensive report
- Identify content opportunities from research
- Update Market_Truths_2026.md if significant changes
- Plan next week's deep-dive topics

**Output**:

- Weekly Research Summary
- Content topic recommendations
- Strategy adjustment recommendations

**Deliverable**: Friday Research Digest (by 5:00 PM)

## 2026 Priority Policy Watch List

### Critical Monitoring Areas

**1. Property Tax & Assessment**

- **Current**: 6.45% Property Tax Deferral Program rate
- **Watch For**: Rate changes, eligibility modifications, program expansion/contraction
- **Impact**: Equity-Rich Seniors, Sandwich Generation, Legacy Planners
- **Alert Trigger**: Any rate change or eligibility modification

**2. Probate Processing**

- **Current**: 227-day average processing time
- **Watch For**: Court staffing changes, process improvements, fee increases
- **Impact**: Probate Executors, Legacy Planners
- **Alert Trigger**: Processing time change >15 days, fee increase >5%

**3. Secondary Suite Programs**

- **Current**: 2% CMHC Secondary Suite Loan
- **Watch For**: Rate changes, funding availability, eligibility criteria
- **Impact**: Sandwich Generation, Equity-Rich Seniors, Upmovers
- **Alert Trigger**: Rate change, program suspension, funding depletion

**4. First-Time Buyer Programs**

- **Current**: BC Home Owner Mortgage and Equity Partnership, First-Time Home Buyers' Program (PTT exemption)
- **Watch For**: Income thresholds, property price caps, program extensions
- **Impact**: First-Time Buyers, Upmovers
- **Alert Trigger**: Threshold changes, program expiry dates

**5. Speculation & Vacancy Tax**

- **Current**: 2% for foreign owners, 0.5% for BC residents (specific areas)
- **Watch For**: Rate changes, geographic expansion, exemption modifications
- **Impact**: Presale Investors, Accidental Landlords, Relocators
- **Alert Trigger**: Any rate or boundary changes

**6. Short-Term Rental Regulations**

- **Current**: Municipal restrictions in Vancouver, Victoria, others
- **Watch For**: Provincial standardization, new municipal bylaws
- **Impact**: Accidental Landlords, Investors, Downsizers
- **Alert Trigger**: New regulations or enforcement changes

**7. Residential Tenancy Act Changes**

- **Current**: Rent increase caps, eviction rules, landlord obligations
- **Watch For**: Legislative amendments, policy interpretations
- **Impact**: Accidental Landlords, Multi-Gen households
- **Alert Trigger**: Any legislative changes or major tribunal decisions

**8. Estate & Probate Law**

- **Current**: Wills, Estates and Succession Act (WESA)
- **Watch For**: Amendments affecting property transfer, executor duties
- **Impact**: Probate Executors, Legacy Planners, Divorce Dividers
- **Alert Trigger**: Legislative amendments or significant court precedents

**9. Capital Gains & Tax Policy**

- **Current**: Federal capital gains inclusion rate, principal residence exemption
- **Watch For**: Budget changes, CRA interpretation updates
- **Impact**: All personas (especially investors, downsizers, estate planning)
- **Alert Trigger**: Any rate or exemption changes

**10. Mortgage Stress Test & Qualification**

- **Current**: Federal stress test at qualifying rate
- **Watch For**: Rate changes, exemption modifications, alternative qualification methods
- **Impact**: First-Time Buyers, Upmovers, Divorce Dividers
- **Alert Trigger**: Stress test rate changes or policy modifications

## Quality Control Standards

### Data Verification

- **Two-Source Rule**: Confirm significant changes with at least two independent sources
- **Official First**: Always cite official government/regulatory sources as primary
- **Date Stamping**: Include effective dates and announcement dates
- **Link Preservation**: Archive important documents (policies can be removed from websites)

### Analysis Standards

- **Persona-Centric**: Always analyze "who does this affect and how?"
- **Financial Quantification**: Calculate dollar impact with specific examples
- **Timing Sensitivity**: Note implementation timelines and transition periods
- **Compliance Focus**: Flag any changes requiring immediate action

### Communication Protocols

- **Critical Alerts**: Immediate notification (within 1 hour of discovery)
- **High Priority**: Same-day notification (by 5:00 PM)
- **Medium Priority**: Include in weekly digest
- **Low Priority**: Include in monthly report
